Youngjae Kim

B.S. Computer Science & Mathematics, University of Maryland.

Hi, I’m YoungjaeYUNG-jeh · 영재, a Computer Science and Mathematics student at the University of Maryland, graduating in May 2027.

I’m interested in distributed systems, infrastructure and system design, and data engineering. What I actually enjoy is the problem solving underneath all of it: working out why something is slow, what a system should do when a dependency goes down, and how to shape data so the next question is easy to answer.

Right now I’m a Software Engineer Intern at Easy Dynamics, building multi-region Azure network infrastructure in Terraform and validating reusable modules against NIST 800-53. Before that I built a retrieval and agent workflow for customer support drafting at Mindgrasp, and spent two years at EchoIT in Seoul on a Java and Spring Boot backend, where a Redis caching layer took P95 API latency from 200 ms to under 30 ms.

Outside of work and class, you’ll usually find me watching a movie, reading, listening to music, or working on calisthenics—pull-ups and pistol squats are a couple of favorites. I’m also thinking about adding a small collection of favorite films and albums here someday.

My resume has the full details, and a few things I’ve built on my own are on the projects page. The best way to reach me is email, either my university address or my personal one.
